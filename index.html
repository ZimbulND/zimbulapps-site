Company & investor overview

Operator perspective. Product focus.

Zimbul Apps LLC is an independent software product company based in Woodbridge, Virginia.

Founder Mor Ndiaye brings more than 20 years of experience in retail operations and multi-unit leadership, including leading teams, managing business performance, and improving processes. That operating background informs how Zimbul Apps identifies problems and develops software.

The company’s direction is a focused portfolio of practical business products: tools for specific workflows, designed around clarity, useful outcomes, and the realities of day-to-day operations.
